Guidelines

Submission instructions, the abstract template, and the length limit will be posted when submission opens. Presenters indicate a preference for a contributed talk or a poster at submission; the organizers select contributed talks from the pool with priority for postdoctoral researchers and senior graduate students.

Attendance

Attendance is capped by the venue. If registrations reach the cap, abstracts received after that point are placed on a waiting list in order of submission.

NASSCC 2027 is hosted by Duquesne University and organized by faculty of Duquesne University, the University of Pittsburgh, and Carnegie Mellon University.

Registration and housing are handled by Duquesne University Conference and Event Services. Questions: Contact.
